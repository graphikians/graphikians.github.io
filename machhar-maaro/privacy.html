Privacy Policy · Effective date: 6 November 2026

Who we are

"Machhar Maaro" (the "Game") is a mobile game published by SANX Graphikians, Navsari, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game uses and why.

In short: We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your own phone. Ads in the Game are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress such as levels, stars, coins, settings, the optional player nickname and avatar you choose, and the optional Secret Diary PIN.
  • This data is saved only in your phone's local storage. We do not upload it to our servers, and we cannot see it.
  • Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded video ads (for example to double coins or revive). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device and app information, and ad interaction data

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more at How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Where required by law, the Game will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional.

Sharing images

When you tap Share, the Game creates an image on your phone and opens your phone's own share menu. Nothing is sent to us. What you share, and with whom, is your choice.

Children

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Security

Because your game data stays on your device, it is protected by your phone's own security. We do not run servers that store player data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
